# AeroTwin logo: prompt for Adobe (Firefly or Illustrator's generative vector tools)

Look of the app: near-black canvas (#09090f), Geist type, hairline borders, rounded cards, pill buttons, one blue accent (#4c8dff), colour only for status. The mark must work at 16 px (favicon), on dark and on light, and as one colour.

## Prompt (paste as is)
Minimal vector logo for "AeroTwin", an aircraft digital twin and maintenance planning product. Concept: a simple airplane seen from above or in side profile, drawn as a single clean outline, with a second identical outline offset beside it like a reflection or a shadow, to say "twin". Geometric, precise, engineered, calm. Built from a few uniform-weight strokes (about 1.5 to 2 px at 24 px size) with rounded joins and rounded terminals, a hairline feel, and generous negative space. Flat vector, no gradients, no glow, no 3D, no shading, no photo texture, no clipart, no globe, no swoosh, no cartoon. Monochrome white on a near-black background (#09090f), with a single accent in blue (#4c8dff) used only on the twin outline or on one small detail. Must stay legible when shrunk to 16 pixels. Centered on a square canvas with even padding. Include a horizontal lockup: the mark on the left and the wordmark "AeroTwin" on the right in a geometric sans-serif like Geist or Inter, medium weight, slight negative letter spacing, with "Aero" and "Twin" the same weight. Give me the mark alone and the lockup.

## Negative prompt (if the tool has the field)
gradient, glow, neon, 3D, drop shadow, photograph, realistic aircraft, jet engine detail, clouds, globe, swoosh, stars, circuit board, robot, brain, hexagon grid, busy detail, thin hairlines that vanish when small, text other than "AeroTwin", misspelled letters

## Three directions to try (swap the concept sentence)
1. **Two outlines:** a top-view airplane whose outline is repeated, shifted a few pixels to the right and down, the second in blue.
2. **A and T monogram:** the letters "A" and "T" formed by the wing and the tail of one plane, the "T" repeated as a mirror image.
3. **Twin wings:** only the wings and tailplane drawn as two parallel chevrons, one white, one blue, pointing up.

## What to export
SVG (first choice), and PNG with a transparent background at 512 and 1024 px: mark alone and horizontal lockup, each in white-on-dark and near-black-on-light. Keep one flat colour version.
